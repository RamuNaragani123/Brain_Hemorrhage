Exploring DL and ML Approaches for Brain Hemorrhage Detection

Emergency Care


Abstract Page

brain hemorrhage detection project, you're implementing a hybrid approach by combining deep learning (DL) and machine learning (ML) techniques, specifically using VGG19 and XGBoost algorithms. VGG19, a deep convolutional neural network, excels at extracting detailed features from medical images such as CT or MRI scans. Its 19-layer architecture allows it to identify subtle patterns associated with brain hemorrhages, making it ideal for medical image classification tasks. By leveraging transfer learning, VGG19 is fine-tuned to your dataset, ensuring high accuracy while maintaining manageable training times.On the other hand, XGBoost is a highly efficient gradient-boosting algorithm known for its performance on structured data. In your case, XGBoost is used to process non-image features, such as patient demographics or clinical data, complementing the visual insights provided by VGG19. The combination of multiple weak learners in XGBoost creates a strong predictive model, which enhances overall classification accuracy.
